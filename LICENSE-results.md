# Licence for aggregated results and figures

The aggregated tables in `results/tables/` and the figures in `results/figures/`
are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

They contain no raw or language-level Ethnologue data. Figures are visualizations
of analyses of the Ethnologue Global and GIS Datasets (SIL International), whose
licence permits publishing visualizations and aggregated results with citation:
Eberhard, D. M., Simons, G. F. & Fennig, C. D. (eds.) 2025. Ethnologue: Languages of
the World, 28th edition. Dallas: SIL International.

Country-level tables derived from the CLIMRISK-SRM research package are released
with the agreement of the CLIMRISK-SRM authors (to be confirmed before publication).
